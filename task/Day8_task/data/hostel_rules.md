
# Hostel Rules and Regulations

## Hostel Accommodation
Students staying in the college hostel must follow the hostel rules and maintain a clean and safe living environment.

## Room Allocation
Rooms are allocated by the hostel administration. Students must stay in their assigned rooms and should not change rooms without permission.

## Attendance and Entry
Students must follow the hostel attendance and entry procedures set by the hostel administration.

## Cleanliness
Students must keep their rooms and common areas clean. Waste should be disposed of in designated bins.

## Safety and Discipline
- Follow instructions from hostel staff.
- Avoid damaging hostel property.
- Report maintenance issues to the hostel office.
- Respect other students and maintain a peaceful environment.

## Important Note
This is sample handbook content for the Day 8 training task, not an official college hostel policy.
