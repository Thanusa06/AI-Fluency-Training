
# Examination Regulations

## Attendance Requirement
Students generally need at least 75% attendance to be eligible to write end-semester examinations.

## Condonation
Students with attendance from 65% up to, but not including, 75% may apply for attendance condonation. The condonation fee is Rs. 500 per course.

## Attendance Below 65%
Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to write the end-semester examination under this sample policy.

## Attendance Validation
Attendance percentage must be between 0 and 100. Values below 0 or above 100 are invalid and must be reported as errors.

## Important Note
This is sample handbook content for the Day 8 training task, not an official college regulation.
