
# College Fee Policy

## Course Fees
The following are sample course fees for the training exercise:
- CS101: Rs. 12,000
- AI202: Rs. 18,000
- DS303: Rs. 15,000

## Late Payment Fee
The maximum late payment fee is Rs. 2,000.

## Late Fee Calculation
For this sample policy, the late fee is Rs. 100 per day, up to a maximum of Rs. 2,000.

Examples:
- 5 days late: Rs. 500
- 10 days late: Rs. 1,000
- 20 days late: Rs. 2,000
- More than 20 days late: Rs. 2,000 maximum

## Total Fee Examples
- CS101 fee plus AI202 fee plus maximum late fee: Rs. 32,000.
- CS101 fee plus AI202 fee plus a 5-day late fee: Rs. 30,500.

## Important Note
These are sample values for the Day 8 training task, not an official college fee schedule.
